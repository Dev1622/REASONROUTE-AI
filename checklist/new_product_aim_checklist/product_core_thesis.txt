PRODUCT CORE THESIS

What we are actually building:
- A Glass Box routing engine for dispatchers.
- A negotiation layer between route planning output and human operational judgment.
- A system that explains route choices, quantifies the cost of overrides, and captures tribal knowledge from dispatcher decisions.

What problem we solve:
- Legacy routing systems are black boxes.
- Dispatchers are forced into a binary choice:
  accept the route as-is, or manually override it.
- When they override, they usually change assignment or sequence without seeing the financial or operational tradeoff.
- This creates the Trust-Profit Gap:
  the company protects SLA decisions in the moment, but silently leaks margin through cost-blind manual changes.

What makes us different:
- We do not replace the dispatcher.
- We do not present the route as a final answer.
- We expose the why, show the cost delta of changes, and turn dispatcher judgment into structured product intelligence.

What the product is not:
- Not just a better route optimizer.
- Not just a dashboard for fleet metrics.
- Not a black-box autopilot for dispatch.
- Not a generic logistics software layer competing feature-for-feature with legacy suites.

The new product identity:
- Glass Box routing
- Dispatcher-first negotiation UI
- Cost-delta visibility
- Tribal knowledge capture
- Human-in-the-loop learning flywheel

The moat:
- Tribal Knowledge Data Moat
- Workflow integration and switching costs
- Trust-first brand position
- Reinforcement loop from dispatcher approval and adjustment behavior
