# ReasonRoute AI Product And Features Overview

## Product Overview

ReasonRoute AI is a cost-aware routing intelligence layer for delivery and fleet operations teams. It sits at the critical decision point between route planning output and dispatcher action, helping teams generate route proposals, understand why a route was chosen, evaluate the cost of changes before they happen, and improve routing decisions over time.

ReasonRoute AI is designed for organizations that do not just need route optimization. They need a system that helps planners and dispatchers make better decisions in the moment, especially when service pressure, local knowledge, and cost tradeoffs collide.

## The Problem We Solve

In many fleet and delivery operations, route plans are frequently adjusted after they are generated. This override culture is not irrational. Dispatchers are often reacting to customer expectations, traffic concerns, local access constraints, service commitments, and practical field knowledge. The problem is that these decisions are often made without a clear view of the full operational and financial tradeoff.

That creates a costly gap:

- The planning system recommends a route
- The dispatcher sees a ETA issue or service risk
- A change gets made quickly
- The business often sees the service outcome, but not always the hidden cost of the decision

This is where avoidable business loss can accumulate through added miles, added time, weaker asset utilization, higher emissions, and missed opportunities to choose a better alternative instead of a fast manual change.

ReasonRoute AI is built to close that gap by creating a decision layer where the system and the dispatcher can effectively "converse" through explanation, tradeoff visibility, and alternative proposals before a costly override is locked in.

## Why This Matters

The business case for a cost-aware intelligence layer is supported by broader logistics research:

- McKinsey wrote that in B2B carriers, about 70% of dispatcher time is dedicated to issues caused by "blind handoffs," and identified communication, dwell time, re-delivery, and manual issue resolution as meaningful sources of waste in logistics operations. This supports the case that poor visibility and reactive coordination create real economic drag, not just operational annoyance. Source: [McKinsey, "Digitizing mid- and last-mile logistics handovers to reduce waste"](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/logistics/our-insights/digitizing-mid-and-last-mile-logistics-handovers-to-reduce-waste)

- BCG reported in 2026 that the last mile now represents more than 50% of total delivery costs in parcel logistics and noted that many carriers still lack real-time insight at the vehicle, route, or driver level. That supports the importance of route-level cost visibility at the point of action. Source: [BCG, "How Cost Intelligence Is Reshaping Parcel Logistics"](https://www.bcg.com/ja-jp/publications/2026/how-cost-intelligence-is-reshaping-parcel-logistics)

- Bain described how routing technology materially affects last-mile cost and showed one example where a dynamically routed model achieved much lower per-unit delivery cost than a more manual traditional route. Source: [Bain, "What We Learned as Last-Mile Delivery Drivers"](https://www.bain.com/insights/what-we-learned-as-last-mile-delivery-drivers/)

- Academic work on logistics planning has also reinforced the value of human-in-the-loop decision support. One 2024 Applied Sciences paper describes transport planners evaluating system recommendations using human expertise beyond what is in the model, and feeding that response back into the planning loop. Source: [Applied Sciences, "A Data-Intelligence-Driven Digital Twin Framework for Improving Sustainability in Logistics"](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/2/601)

Taken together, these sources do not prove a single universal "override cost" number for every fleet. But they do support the central operating truth behind ReasonRoute AI: when route decisions are made under pressure without explanation and cost visibility, companies are more likely to absorb hidden inefficiency and miss better alternatives.

## What ReasonRoute AI Is

ReasonRoute AI combines three product roles in one platform:

- A route planning and fleet proposal system
- A dispatcher-facing review and decision console
- A cost-aware intelligence layer for route changes and operational tradeoffs

## Core Product Capabilities

### 1. Route Planning And Fleet Proposal Generation

Rather than operating as a traditional "black box" where routing decisions are hidden, ReasonRoute AI acts as a transparent "glass box" layer. This allows dispatchers to see exactly what is happening inside the engine and fully understand the reasoning behind a route, transforming multi-stop operational planning from a rigid, fixed output into a clear, reviewable proposal.

### 2. Explainable "Why This Route" Guidance

Each route proposal includes a plain-language explanation of why the route was selected. This helps users understand route structure, stop order logic, corridor choices, and efficiency factors without relying on technical logs or opaque optimization output.

### 3. Cost-Aware Override Intelligence

Before a dispatcher confirms a route adjustment, ReasonRoute AI can show the likely tradeoff of that change across key planning dimensions such as time, distance, emissions, and cost-oriented impact where available. This turns the override moment into an informed business decision rather than a blind operational reaction.

### 4. Dispatcher Review And Approval

Routes are reviewed before finalization. Dispatchers can inspect the recommendation, approve it, or request a change. This keeps human judgment central while improving consistency and decision quality.

### 5. Adjustment Requests And Updated Proposals

If a user wants a route changed, ReasonRoute AI supports adjustment requests and produces an updated proposal. Users can compare the current and adjusted route proposals so they can see what changed and what that change means operationally.

### 6. Tribal Knowledge Capture

Dispatchers can attach structured adjustment reasons using quick tags and notes. This helps preserve operational knowledge such as access constraints, local traffic patterns, customer-specific realities, and routing preferences in a reusable form.

### 7. Blocked Corridor And Exception Handling

Users can mark blocked roads or route exceptions and request a revised route proposal. The updated route and its explanation reflect the change so the reason for the reroute is visible and easy to review.

### 8. Real-World Data Intake

The platform supports operational data import from common business formats including CSV, XLSX, and JSON. This makes it easier for teams to bring real planning data into a structured route decision workflow.

### 9. Baseline And Historical Outcome Comparison

ReasonRoute AI can compare route proposals against baseline planning results or historical outcomes. This helps organizations evaluate not only route efficiency, but also whether different routing decisions may have introduced avoidable cost or missed better alternatives.

### 10. Driver-Facing Route Explanation Output

The product can create short, plain-language route explanation text for route handoff and manifest-style outputs. This extends clarity beyond the planning desk and helps reduce confusion around route changes.

### 11. Reporting And Decision Evidence

ReasonRoute AI produces route summaries and decision evidence that can be used in operational reviews, customer discussions, pilot assessments, and stakeholder conversations. This helps organizations discuss route quality and decision quality with more credibility.

### 12. Exportable Route And Decision Artifacts

The product can export route summaries, manifests, route geometry, and structured decision context in shareable formats so teams can review outcomes across operations and leadership groups.

## Business Value

ReasonRoute AI helps organizations:

- Reduce cost-blind route overrides
- Improve visibility into the tradeoffs behind route changes
- Preserve dispatcher knowledge instead of losing it in manual edits
- Improve trust in planning recommendations through explanation
- Identify where a better alternative exists between the original route and the first manual override
- Strengthen routing conversations with customers, operations leaders, and investors using evidence instead of intuition alone

## Best Fit

ReasonRoute AI is best suited for logistics, delivery, distribution, and fleet operations teams that want:

- explainable route planning
- cost-aware route decision support
- a dispatcher-first review experience
- stronger visibility into route-change tradeoffs
- a more intelligent bridge between planning systems and human operational judgment
